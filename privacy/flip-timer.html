Flip Timer — Privacy Policy

Package com.fliptimer.app · 1.0.0 · Effective 2026-11-20

In one line

Flip Timer collects no personal data. The app holds no internet permission, so nothing can leave your device.

Data we collect

None. We do not collect or transmit names, email addresses, phone numbers, location, contacts, photos, or device identifiers. There is no account and no sign-in.

What stays on your device

The four side durations, your theme / typeface / layout choice, and the alert mode (vibrate, sound, flash, silent) are stored in the app's own storage on your device. They are never transmitted, and they are removed when you uninstall the app.

Permissions

Only VIBRATE, used to buzz the phone when a timer ends. The accelerometer is read to tell which side faces the ground; Android requires no permission for it and the readings are never recorded.

Ads and analytics

There are no ads. No analytics or tracking SDK is included.

Children

Because no data is collected, no data from children under 13 is collected either.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page is updated and the effective date above is revised.
